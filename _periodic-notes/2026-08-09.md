## New synthesis: Self-Report Is Not Verification

Created [[Self-Report Is Not Verification]] in `concepts/ai-development/`.

The orphan audit surfaced that [[The Honesty Gap]], [[Thinking Out Loud]] and [[Teach the Delta]] were all missing from the MOC, and reading them together made an unstated shared claim obvious: **a model's confidence in its own output is drawn from the same distribution that produced the output, so the errors are correlated and the self-assessment carries no independent information.** That single claim explains several results the KB already held separately —

- why [[The Honesty Gap]]'s forced blanks and EXTRACTED/INFERRED labels work where confidence scores don't
- why [[Simplified Technical English (STE)]]'s *linter* cut slop 74% while a banned-words list in the prompt moved Claude 3%
- why [[Evaluating Skills]] insists on evals over vibe checks, and what actually makes LLM-as-judge trustworthy (independence from the generating process, not being non-model)
- why [[Thinking Out Loud]]'s self-rating trick is safe — the number is a *trigger*, not a *truth*

Backlinked contextually from all four, plus [[Harnesses Matter More Than Models]] at the linter-feedback-loop paragraph. Added to the MOC under Synthesis, indexed under a new **Verification & Trust** cluster, and logged in [[SAGE Synthesis]].

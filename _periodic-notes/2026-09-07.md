## New note: Knowledge Trails

Wrote [[Knowledge Trails]] in `concepts/ai-development/`. The argument:

- The mission and the hands-on frustration are non-transferable; the technique is transferable, and mostly isn't transferred.
- The reason is structural — **the acquisition process destroys the evidence.** Once a shift lands it re-files as baseline, so you stop mentioning it. That's the inverse of [[Teach the Delta]]: teaching the delta needs you to know where your baseline *was*, and only a record kept at the time preserves that.
- A tip is the technique flat; a trail is the technique plus trigger, date and ordering. The trigger is the part a reader recognises themselves in.
- Same stigmergy pattern as [[Contextual Breadcrumbs]], one timescale up — with the difficulty inverted, since a human has to come looking rather than having the breadcrumb pushed to them.
- The publishing observation: a vault has a concept axis (`concepts/` + `_MOCs/`) and a time axis (`_periodic-notes/`). Sharing a KB usually means sharing the concept axis — which is exactly the axis with the trigger polished out. **The trail is the time axis.**
